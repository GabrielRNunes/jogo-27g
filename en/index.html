Welcome

On the morning of the world final, Kairo is missing. While inspecting the player’s private suite, you trigger containment and become trapped. Gather clues across five rooms, recover the exit PIN and investigate what happened. You have five minutes before security takes over.

Start investigating

Game highlights

  • Five connected rooms, ten puzzles and an inventory, with objects that interact across different rooms.
  • Sequence and memory minigames with keyboard, click or touch controls, randomized sequences each run, and an investigation log that records clues.
  • Four endings based on time, mistakes and discoveries.
