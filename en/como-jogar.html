How to Play

Step by step

  1. Open Play Now and read the briefing.
  2. Select Start and follow the introduction (or select Skip story). The five-minute countdown only starts when the introduction ends.
  3. Select objects to inspect them. Select doors to move between rooms.
  4. For larger buttons, open Objects and connections in this room, below the scene. You can also click or tap the on-screen arrows on the command panel.
  5. Collect items, select them in your inventory and use them on the right objects. Only one item can be selected at a time.
  6. Use the Investigation log to review clues. Newest entries appear first, with their room and the time remaining when found. The optional calculator on the Training PC explains each binary group and pauses the countdown while open. Collapsing it or closing the panel resumes the countdown.
  7. To listen to the soundtrack, open Ambient sound (optional) above the game and press play. Audio and the investigation log do not pause the countdown.
  8. Reprogram the Exit Corridor lock before time runs out.

Rules

Time
Five minutes of active countdown. The binary calculator is the only pause, provided as a learning aid; other panels do not stop time. At zero, security takes over the suite and the run ends with Caught.
Mistakes
Each incorrect code or wrong wire in the fuse box costs 10 seconds and contributes to your ending. Some mistakes also affect other objects.
Dependencies
Some actions unlock objects in other rooms. Others only reveal code clues. The Items page distinguishes clues from required actions.

Frequently asked questions

Can I play with a keyboard only?

Yes. Doors and objects are buttons you can focus with Tab and activate with Enter. Panels close with Esc and game messages are marked for screen readers to announce.
