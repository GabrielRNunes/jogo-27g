Mission briefing

Loading controls. If this message remains, reload the page and check that JavaScript is enabled in an up-to-date browser.

The world final starts at 6 p.m., but Kairo is missing. You are the IT technician sent to inspect his private suite. Your badge permits entry, but the missing occupant permission triggers containment. The service exit is locked.

Explore five rooms, collect clues and reprogram the lock before security takes over. You have five minutes; invalid codes and wrong wires cost ten seconds. Your discoveries, time and mistakes determine the ending.

How to play

Use the mouse or Tab and Enter to interact. Select inventory items before using them. The countdown starts after you finish or skip the introduction. During a run, opening the binary calculator pauses time; collapsing it or closing the panel resumes the countdown. Other panels do not pause.

Switching languages restarts the game.
